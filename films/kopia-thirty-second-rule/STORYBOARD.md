# Storyboard — Kopia · The Thirty-Second Rule

Theme: 087 Cut-Paper Title Sequence (adapted: warm cut-paper dioramas, 029's on-twos boil) · Format: 1080×1920 ·
Length: 37 s · Sound: original 120 BPM Afro-pop bed + coded SFX + 5 synthetic voice lines (Kokoro), all dialogue subtitled

## Message & tone
- **Sentence:** I want to say "Your hard work, perfectly presented" in a warm, cinematic tone, so the viewer feels
  that their own messy, hard-won work deserves to look this good, and that Kopia is how.
- **Tone arc:** urgent → mysterious → bold → trustworthy → warm → celebratory
- accent: blue
- **Visual thesis:** the whole world is cut paper. Everything made by hand is rough: torn edges that *boil* (a seeded
  jitter on twos). Everything Kopia prints is crisp: clean straight edges that never move. The proof moment is the
  instant the boil stops.
- **Motif:** `motif:the-copy`, the second copy. Two copies leave the printer (offset, one behind the other), the
  sleeve holds both, the second one goes to Maman, and on the end card the logo's red tile slides out from behind the
  K tile the same way: the copy is the brand.

## The seven questions (answered)
1. **What and how:** the sentence above. Warm because every scene is a person and a piece of paper; cinematic
   because it moves through four places with real light changes (classroom, night, shop, golden-hour market).
2. **Repeats:** audited. Deliberate: `motif:the-copy`. Match-cut, push-in and rack-focus are each used twice, never
   back to back.
3. **Transition meaning:** match-cut = the same paper in a new hand (cause → effect). Whip-pan / rack-focus = the
   lecturer's attention moving. Hard-cut into the blackout = the night arrives without warning. Time-lapse = hours of
   work. Light-leak = dawn. Screen-handoff = the order leaves her phone and lands in the shop. Push-through = we enter
   the work. Beat-cut = the music takes over. Sleeve-wipe = the package leaves the shop. Copy-carry = the second copy
   travels home.
4. **Colour over time:** kraft, cream, chalk green and umber only for 13 s. Navy is the night. Blue arrives on the
   phone at 13.0 (colour event 1: Kopia), lives in the shop as headings and the printer light, and floods the frame on
   the end card (colour event 2). Red appears once, as the logo's copy tile.
5. **New component:** *print-seam*. Verb: transforms. Metaphor: a print head passing over a page. Primitive:
   document rows. Twist: above the seam the paper still boils and the ink is handwriting; below it every line is set
   in type and **stops moving**. Truth check: Kopia's typing service turns handwritten pages into typed ones. Motion
   signature: the seam steps down one line per eighth note (`steps(12)`), each line snapping still.
6. **Surprise:** 7.5 s blackout + silence; 20.0 s the drop where the boil stops; 34.0 s the copy becomes the logo.
7. **Last films:** the Remotion ad (chat phone, coin lock, scooter map, PIN card, photo cards, circle wipe) and
   Reste en cours (overhead desk rig, margin tabs, yellow flood CTA, dolly-out). None of those devices return. The
   phone appears once, with one order card.

## Frames

### Frame 1 · Hook (0–7 s)
- key visual: a messy plan stack slams onto the desk; Dr. Mbarga holds a page like evidence; the board says FRIDAY 10:00 · TYPED. PRINTED.
- moves first: the stack, from above, `power4.in`
- on-screen words: board words (in-world) + subtitles

### Frame 2 · Hook, the night (7–13 s)
- key visual: the lamp dies, the phone torch carries her through 18 handwritten pages; morning shows a mess
- on-screen words: "Great ideas. Messy pages."

### Frame 3 · Reveal (13–15 s)
- key visual: she photographs the pages and orders on kopia.online; the first blue of the film
- on-screen words: "Order on kopia.online"

### Frame 4 · Proof moment (15–26 s)
- key visual: the print-seam: handwriting above, still clean type below; the printer drop; two crisp copies; PIN handover
- new component: print-seam
- on-screen words: "Handwritten → typed and formatted." · "Delivered to your classroom. Payment released at handover."

### Frame 5 · Payoff (26–34 s)
- key visual: Maman wipes her hands, holds up the second copy; the cash box; "Your first investor."

### Frame 6 · CTA (34–37 s)
- key visual: KOPIA Blue, the K tile drops, the red copy slides out, KOPIA, "Your hard work, perfectly presented." · kopia.online
- loop note: not a loop; the last second is a living hold (micro drift)
