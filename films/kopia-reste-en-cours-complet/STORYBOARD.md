# Storyboard — Kopia · Reste en cours (version complète)

Theme: 099 Overhead Desk · Format: 1080×1920 · Length: 40 s · Sound: French voice-over (11 lines) + 120 BPM marimba/kalimba bed (ducked under the voice, stop-time on the stamps) + warm tuned SFX

## Message & tone
- **Sentence:** I want to say "stay in class, Kopia handles everything from the order to the handover" in a warm, trustworthy tone, so the viewer feels they can trust a stranger with their money and their course pack.
- **Tone arc:** urgent → bold → trustworthy → trustworthy → warm → playful → warm
- accent: blue
- **Motif:** the official Kopia mark. It lands on the desk on the drop, becomes the wax seal of the money envelope, marks the margin tabs, and its blue finally floods the frame. The camera rig, the courier's blue sleeve and the dolly-out are carried over from the 15 s film on purpose (same world, longer story).

## The seven questions (answered)
1. **What and how:** the sentence above; warm because it never leaves the student's world, trustworthy because the money is visibly sealed until the PIN.
2. **Repeats:** audited; carried-over devices from the 15 s film are marked `motif:`.
3. **Transitions:** dolly-out = you are one of many, still served; object carry = the brand becomes the seal of trust; push-in = look at the money; beat-cut-hold = silence makes the prices land; match cut = paper becomes typed paper; parallax slide = the next desk; graphic match = the blue cover becomes the message.
4. **Colour:** wood/paper/ink; Kopia blue appears on the drop, the envelope, the tabs, the courier; red only as the wax seal and the mark's offset; blue floods at the end.
5. **New components:** escrow envelope with a PIN seal, price stamps, typed sheet, referral note.
6. **Surprises:** 4 s (hall), 19.5 s (stop-time stamps), 31 s (next desk).
7. **Last films:** no coin, lock, PIN card, route map or split bars from the first ad.
