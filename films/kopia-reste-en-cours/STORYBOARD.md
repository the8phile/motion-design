# Storyboard — Kopia · Reste en cours

Theme: 099 Overhead Desk · Format: 1080×1920 · Length: 15 s · Sound: music (120 BPM, warm marimba/kalimba bed) + warm tuned SFX

## Message & tone
- **Sentence:** I want to say "Restez en cours : vos impressions viennent à vous" in a warm, human tone, so the viewer feels relieved that printing no longer costs them a lecture.
- **Tone arc:** urgent → urgent → bold → trustworthy → warm
- accent: blue
- **Motif:** the official Kopia mark (blue tile, white K, red offset). It is set down on the desk on the drop (reveal), slides to the notebook margin and becomes the clip of the margin tabs (proof), returns as the tag on the delivered course pack, and finally its blue floods the frame as the CTA field. Same meaning every time ("Kopia is handling it"), growing from a sticker to the whole screen.

## The seven questions (answered)
1. **What and how:** the sentence above. Warm because the whole film stays at a student's desk: paper, wood, a pen, a hand. The pain (urgent) is short; the relief (trustworthy, then warm) gets the time.
2. **Repeats:** audited. The only deliberate repeats are the K tag (motif) and the hard cut inside the hook (motif:cut).
3. **Transition meaning:** *motif:cut* = the thought becomes action, no ceremony. *dolly-out-reveal* = the drop: "you are one of hundreds in this lecture, and Kopia still finds your seat". *object-carry* = the K tag walks into the notebook: Kopia is now part of the lesson. *graphic-match-color* = the blue tag on the delivered pack becomes the whole message.
4. **Colour over time:** wood, paper and ink only in the hook. Kopia blue first appears on the Commander button (a small spark), then once as the mark on the drop (colour event 1); the courier wears blue; the brand red only lives in the mark's offset. Blue floods the frame at the CTA (colour event 2) with the official white logo.
5. **New component:** *margin tabs*. Verb: delivers. Metaphor: index tabs in a binder. Primitive: status chips. Twist: the order's progress is measured in lines of the student's own notes, so the viewer sees that studying never stopped. Truth check: the shop prints, a verified courier delivers to the classroom, the student is notified; no times or numbers invented. Motion signature: each tab slides out of the margin from behind the page with `power2.out` in 0.3 s, its clip snapping shut one frame later.
6. **Surprise:** the dolly-out on the drop at 4.0 s (scale jump from one desk to the whole hall). 15 s film: one surprise is enough.
7. **Last film:** the first Kopia ad (Remotion) used a chat phone, a coin into a lock, a scooter route map, a PIN card, photo cards, a circle wipe and crossfades. None of those appear here; the phone only shows one order card and one notification.

## Frames

### Frame 1 · Hook (0–2 s)
- key visual: a lecture-hall desk seen from straight above; notebook open, pen, phone showing 09:58
- moves first: the pen writes "imprimer le chap. 4 ?" left to right, then a nervous scribble circles it
- on-screen words: "imprimer le chap. 4 ?" (handwritten, in-world)
- beat hook: the circle closes on beat 4

### Frame 2 · Hook, the action (2–4 s)
- key visual: the student's hand slides the phone up; the Kopia order card: chapitre-4.pdf · 24 p · Commander
- moves first: the hand, from the bottom edge, `expo.out`
- on-screen words: "chapitre-4.pdf · 24 p", "Commander"
- beat hook: the thumb tap on the last beat before the drop

### Frame 3 · Reveal (4–5.5 s)
- key visual: the camera pulls up: dozens of desks in rows, a lecturer's desk at the top, our desk with the blue Kopia mark the only colour; "Restez en cours."
- moves first: the K tag drops onto the desk on the downbeat, then the camera rises
- on-screen words: "Restez en cours." (3 words)

### Frame 4 · Proof moment (5.5–11.5 s)
- key visual: the notebook fills with notes while margin tabs pop: Imprimé ✓ · Coursier vérifié · À la porte; then a courier's hand sets the stapled course pack on the desk; the phone says "Livré à votre place"
- new component: margin tabs
- moves first: the K tag sliding to the margin (object carry), then the pen
- on-screen words: the three tabs (2 words each) + the notification (3 words)

### Frame 5 · CTA (11.5–15 s)
- key visual: Kopia blue field, official white logo, white type: "Vos impressions viennent à vous." · kopia.online · "dès 25 XAF la page · livraison en salle 200 XAF"
- loop note: not a loop; the last second is a living hold
